Digital Compass Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 31 August 2026

If you connected a Facebook Page or eligible Instagram professional account to Digital Compass through Meta authorization, you may request deletion of data associated with that connection.

Option 1 — Revoke access in Meta

  1. Open your Facebook or Meta account settings.
  2. Go to the section that lists apps, websites, or business integrations connected to your account.
  3. Locate Digital Compass Social Manager.
  4. Remove or revoke its access.

Revoking access prevents Digital Compass from using the authorization for future account-management actions.

Option 2 — Request deletion from Digital Compass

Send a deletion request through the contact method published on the Digital Compass website and identify the Facebook Page or Instagram professional account that was connected. We may request reasonable verification that the requester is authorized to act for that business account.

What we delete

After a verified deletion request, Digital Compass will delete or anonymize stored connection records, access-token references, authorization metadata, and other data associated with the connected account unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

Timing

Verified deletion requests are processed within a reasonable period. The connected account may become unavailable for Digital Compass management immediately after authorization is revoked.
