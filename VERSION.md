# Digital Compass — Website Edition

This branch is the independent Website Edition of Digital Compass.

## Editions

- `main` — Social Media Edition (social-only operating product)
- `website-version` — Website + Social Edition (website creation, prototypes, discovery, audit, outreach, automation, and social management)

The Website Edition is intentionally preserved from the pre-social-only baseline so changes to the Social Media Edition do not remove its website/prototype capabilities.

## Important

The two editions are treated as separate product surfaces. Do not apply social-only removals to this branch.
