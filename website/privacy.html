Digital Compass Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Digital Compass provides social-media management tools that allow an authorized business owner or administrator to connect Facebook Pages and eligible Instagram professional accounts through Meta's official authorization flow.

Information we process

When a user authorizes a Meta connection, Digital Compass may process account and Page identifiers, Page names, Instagram professional account identifiers, permissions granted by the user, access tokens required to perform authorized actions, messages or comments made available through the permissions granted, publishing data, and operational logs needed to deliver and secure the service.

How we use information

We use this information only to provide requested account-management functions such as reading permitted Page information, managing posts, comments or messages when authorized, connecting eligible Instagram professional accounts, operating approved publishing workflows, and maintaining security and audit records.

Access tokens and security

Access tokens are stored server-side and are not intentionally exposed in the public website or client-side application. We use access controls and secret storage mechanisms intended to reduce unauthorized access.

Sharing

Digital Compass does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the service, including Meta Platforms and our hosting/database infrastructure, only as necessary for the requested functionality and subject to their applicable terms.

Data retention

We retain connected-account data only for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Authorization data may be removed earlier when a connection is revoked or a deletion request is completed.

User choices

Users can revoke Digital Compass access through their Meta account settings. Revoking access may prevent further management actions. Users can also request deletion of data associated with their Meta authorization by following our Data Deletion Instructions.

Third-party platforms

Use of Facebook and Instagram is also subject to Meta's own terms, policies and privacy practices. Digital Compass does not control Meta's independent processing of information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy or deletion questions, contact Digital Compass through the contact method published on our main website.
